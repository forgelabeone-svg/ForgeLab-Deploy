## Features

- Persian RTL UI with Vazirmatn font (keeping brand terms like AI, PRO, XP, Avatar, Scenario, Future Me, Dashboard, Insight, Streak, Achievement, Challenge, Timeline in English)
- Landing page with hero ('آینده‌ات را قبل از زندگی کردن، ببین.'), interactive AI Core/Avatar visual, and dual CTAs
- Multi-step onboarding (name, age, job, income, skills, goals, interests, habits, concerns, risk tolerance, life priorities) with live AI profile building
- AI Personality Engine storing personality, goals, values, risk profile, decision style, financial behavior, career direction, habits, strengths, weaknesses, interests with session-to-session memory
- Main Dashboard with Future Score, Level, XP, Streak, Daily Scenario, Recent Decisions, AI Insights, Future Timeline, and personalized daily greeting
- Daily Scenario Engine generating personalized scenarios with A/B/C choices and consequence analysis across Finance, Career, Stress, Time, Relationships, Growth, Risk
- Decision Engine with animated score results
- What If Engine producing 3 scenarios (A/B/C) with timelines at 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years covering Career, Finance, Lifestyle, Risk, Opportunity, Potential Outcome
